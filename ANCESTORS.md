# Ancestors — Where Am I From?

Faithbook's Ancestors page is a private, source-aware family-history workspace.

## Core principle
Every person matters. Ancestry can help explain family history, place, migration, work, language, and tradition, but it does not determine spiritual worth, intelligence, destiny, holiness, or belonging.

## Records
A person record can store:
- name and alternate/birth names
- birth/death date or year
- birth place
- places lived and migration route
- work, occupations, service, and crafts
- languages
- family traditions, recipes, faith communities, and practices
- family story / notes
- source and citation
- confidence label
- an optional external genealogy reference

Relationship records can distinguish biological parent, adoptive parent, guardian/caregiver, step-parent, and other family links.

## Confidence labels
- DOCUMENTED
- PROBABLE
- FAMILY_TRADITION
- DISPUTED
- UNKNOWN

Uncertainty is preserved instead of silently converted into fact.

## Privacy
Records are designed for app-private local storage. Raw DNA import is not part of V18. The page does not require racial categories. Users should not publish living relatives' private information without permission.

## Biblical genealogy study
The page links to biblical genealogy starting points such as Genesis 5, Genesis 10, Genesis 11, Ruth 4, 1 Chronicles 1–9, Matthew 1, and Luke 3. These are Scripture-study references only. Faithbook does not infer that a modern user descends from a biblical person.

## Naming
Where the app presents a KJV naming lens, it is metadata/reference only. It never rewrites the stored Scripture text.
