# Ancestry Privacy Note

Family-history data can identify living people and family relationships. Faithbook V18 treats Ancestors records as private local study data by design.

The V18 page does not require a user's race, ethnicity, or raw genetic file. External genealogy references are optional text references only. Raw DNA/genome importing is intentionally not implemented in this release.

Before sharing/exporting ancestry material, users should remove information that living relatives have not agreed to publish.
