# Arthur Bible Atlas — Public Arkforge Faith Edition

Arthur Bible Atlas is the research layer around Arkforge Faith Scripture. It organizes names, languages, people, peoples, places, objects, maps, timelines, parables, miracles, teachings, Church roles, traditions, and source-labeled commentary.

## Seven Commentary Leaves
1. Text + Translation
2. Names + Language
3. People + Peoples + Relationships
4. Places + Maps + Environment
5. Objects + Institutions + Daily Life
6. Timeline + Historical Setting
7. Theology + Interpretations + Resolution

Each leaf may expand without a fixed length. Uncertainty and disagreement remain visible. Scripture, official Church teaching, history, scholarship, Arthur synthesis, and personal notes are labeled rather than blended.

## Arthur's role
Arthur is a visible study guide and curator. Jesus remains the center of the app. Arthur points back to Scripture, sources, study tools, and appropriate clergy/teachers where useful.
