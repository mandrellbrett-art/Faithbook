# Arkforge Faith — Bible Library

## Built-in Scripture foundation
The app can install the configured 73-book Catholic Original Douay-Rheims dataset into app-private local storage for offline reading and search.

## Customer imports
Customers may attach or import their own rights-cleared/public-domain study documents through Android's document picker. Imported material remains local unless the customer explicitly exports or shares it.

## Rights rule
Availability online is not permission to redistribute. Arkforge Faith does not ship third-party proprietary study notes, unauthorized scans, or DRM-circumvented material.
