# HeritageFaith V19 Foundation Architecture

## Surface
**Read → Understand → Live**. The surface is intentionally quiet and non-gamified.

## Truth / authority separation
1. Scripture
2. Church teaching / doctrine
3. Sacred Tradition and liturgy
4. Historical and linguistic scholarship
5. Saints, Fathers, and witnesses
6. Study commentary
7. Personal notes and reflections

A lower layer may illuminate a higher layer but never silently overwrite it.

## Research stack
`Source → Context → Ademic Cantus → Resonance → Symbol/Runic Compression → Recursive Eye → Discernment → Plain-language expansion`

Every constructed symbol must remain reversible. The system preserves disagreements and uncertainty rather than recursively forcing a preferred conclusion.

## Creation stack
Material → Biological → Ecological → Human → Symbolic → Interior → Moral → Theological. Science and theology may be compared but are not collapsed into one evidence category.

## Preservation
Constructor owns backend file/project navigation, manifests, hashes, status, provenance, import/export routing, unresolved-reference visibility, and rollback discipline. No migration is promoted merely because a new UI launches.
