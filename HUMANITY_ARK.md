# Humanity Ark

Humanity Ark is Faithbook's expandable context library for the human and natural world surrounding Scripture.

It is not a replacement Bible, not an encyclopedia claiming final completeness, and not a hidden-code system.

## Current V17 expanded starter collections
- **74** named People & Work records
- **30** Peoples & Groups records
- **25** Plants & Their Uses records
- **20** Biblical Foods

- **10** Textiles & Fibers

- **20** Materials & Tools

## Entry rules
Every entry should be able to carry KJV name/wording where applicable, modern identification, Scripture references, use/function, historical context, confidence/dispute status, named contribution, source/provenance, and notes.

## Credit rule
Faithbook credits a person only for work actually attributed by a cited source. A biblical statement such as “father of” or “instructor of” is not treated as private ownership of a human skill. Skills and vocations remain learnable by other people.

## Scientific and historical humility
A biblical word is not automatically identical to a modern species, ethnic category, profession, or material category. Translation history, historical context, and modern identification remain separate where necessary.

## Purpose
The goal is to help a reader encounter Scripture with more of its lived world visible: peoples, food, work, clothing, trade, plants, animals, tools, worship, family, law, art, governance, craft, travel, materials, and ordinary human life.
