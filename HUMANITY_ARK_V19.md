# Humanity Ark V19

The Humanity Ark preserves how people live, work, make, grow, repair, teach, discover, serve, and transmit knowledge. Every person has equal dignity; vocation, ancestry, class, office, or technological capability never creates a superior human category.

Domains include foods; oils/aromatics; plants; animals/husbandry; textiles; manufacturing; infrastructure; fuel/energy; anatomy; biology; scientific and biblical Tree of Life layers; stonemasons/builders; artists/makers; military service; historical knights/chivalry; police/public safety; civilian life; governance; agriculture; labor/trades; aristocratic/noble historical systems; patents/inventions; technology; family gifts/traditions; and human techniques.

**Credit follows contribution.** Records should preserve who did what, when, with which source/evidence, and under which license or permission.
