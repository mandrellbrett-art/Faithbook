# HeritageFaith — Privacy Policy Draft (V19)

HeritageFaith is designed as a local-first study application. Personal notes, prayer notes, ancestry records, family gifts/traditions, study records, and invention records are intended to remain on the customer's device unless the customer explicitly exports or shares them.

HeritageFaith should not require camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, call-log, or raw-DNA access for its core Scripture and research experience. Optional platform features, if later added, must be disclosed and separately permissioned.

The retail app must not ship with developer/owner private information. Ancestry features do not require racial categories or raw DNA data and should warn users not to publish living relatives' private information without permission.

Final store publication requires a jurisdiction-appropriate privacy review and a Google Play Data Safety declaration matching the actual shipped binary. This file is a draft, not legal advice.
