# HeritageFaith V19 — Public Scope

HeritageFaith is a public, local-first Christian study app. Core public scope includes Scripture study, source-labeled context, Jesus study, Church/tradition study, Humanity Ark, Ancestors, Family Gifts, Patents/Inventions, Technology, Alexandria, Map of Creation, Ademic Cantus/Resonance, runes/sigils research, the Human Art of Meaning course, human techniques, private notes, and Constructor navigation.

The public product does **not** bundle private Home Base data, owner continuity archives, personal medical/contact/location records, private ancestry, private photos, user-owned copyrighted books, proprietary study-Bible notes, or unlicensed scans.

Target business model: **$4.99 one-time purchase**, no required subscription and no ads.
