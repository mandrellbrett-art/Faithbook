# HeritageFaith V19 Foundation

HeritageFaith is a local-first, Scripture-first Christian study application with a quiet paper-and-leather interface. V19 turns the accumulated design into a working source foundation while keeping experimental research layers visibly separate from Scripture.

## Core navigation

**READ** opens Scripture. **UNDERSTAND** opens context, Jesus study, Church/tradition study, the Map of Creation, Alexandria, and Humanity Ark. **LIVE** opens worksheets, vocation/service, family gifts, ancestors, human techniques, prayer notes, and private study records.

## V19 foundation modules

- Scripture reader + Scripture Context Atlas + Seven Commentary Leaves
- Jesus Center, Living Church, Living Tradition, 300 Faith Worksheets
- Humanity Ark: foods, oils, plants, animals, textiles, manufacturing, infrastructure, fuel, anatomy, biology, Tree of Life, vocations, governance, agriculture, labor, patents, technology, and human techniques
- Ancestors + Family Gifts & Traditions
- Patents & Inventions provenance registry
- Technology Continuum + custom evidence-labeled technology degrees
- Library of Alexandria research shell
- Map of Creation + eight Layers of Creation
- Ademic Cantus + Resonance + reversible generated Ademic runes
- historical-runes/sigil foundation kept separate from constructed Ademic notation
- The Human Art of Meaning course
- Occult & Esoteric source research with Recursive Eye truth testing
- Constructor backend navigator

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Scripture is not rewritten. Source, doctrine, tradition, scholarship, interpretation, speculation, and personal reflection remain separately labeled. Ademic Cantus and Ademic runes are original notation systems, not ancient or hidden biblical code. Supernatural claims remain claims unless independently established. Human dignity does not depend on ancestry, vocation, class, gifts, technology level, or authority.

Private Home Base material is preserved under `legacy_private_reference/` only as development history and is excluded from the compiled public app source tree. Historical public assets removed from the runtime are preserved under `legacy_public_reference/`.
