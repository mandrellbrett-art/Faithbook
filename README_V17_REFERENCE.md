# Faithbook V17 — Humanity Ark

Faithbook is the public, local-first Scripture study app branch.

## V17 direction
Faithbook is intentionally quiet: plain paper, aged-leather framing, restrained ink, calligraphic headings, and minimal motion. It is designed as a study companion rather than a reward system.

The three primary doors are:
- **Read** — Scripture itself.
- **Understand** — context and reference material around Scripture.
- **Live** — prayer, church study, worksheets, vocation, service, and private notes.

## Scripture rule
**Do not alter Scripture.**
The configured 73-book Scripture foundation is rendered as received. Commentary, notes, questions, maps, historical context, naming references, and Church study material are separate layers.

## Humanity Ark
V17 adds an expandable Humanity Ark with:
- named biblical people and work credited to them where the text attributes it,
- plants and their uses,
- foods,
- oils, ointments, and aromatics,
- textiles and fibers including linen, wool, silk, and an explicit caution that standard KJV wording does not simply name cotton,
- animals with translation/identification caveats where needed,
- traditions and practices,
- a 16-domain Civilization Garden of learnable skills and vocations.

## KJV naming lens
Faithbook uses familiar King James Version English names/spellings as reference metadata where applicable. This **never rewrites the underlying Bible text**. Modern names and uncertain identifications remain separately labeled.

## Credit
Faithbook is designed to preserve contributor and source credit. User-created notes belong to the user and stay private by default unless explicitly shared/exported.

## Retail status
Target price: **$4.99 one-time purchase**.

Current status: **source candidate only**. A signed Google Play AAB, physical-device acceptance, final rights/privacy review, hosted privacy policy, Data Safety declaration, and Play approval are still required.

## V17 Humanity Ark expansion
The current starter now covers named biblical contributors and work, peoples/groups, plants, foods, oils, textiles, animals, materials/tools, traditions, and 22 learnable Civilization Garden domains. Coverage remains explicitly incomplete and expandable.
