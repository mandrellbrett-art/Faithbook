# Arkforge Faith — Rights and Scripture Sources

## Built-in Scripture foundation
**Original Douay-Rheims Bible (1582 NT; 1609–1610 OT)**

Dataset repository: https://github.com/janvier-s/original-douay-rheims
License file: https://github.com/janvier-s/original-douay-rheims/blob/main/LICENSE

The repository identifies the complete structured Bible dataset as **CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain** and says it may be used freely with no attribution required. Arkforge still records the source for provenance.

Arkforge includes the 73 books of the Catholic canon by default. The repository's three appendix texts (3 Esdras, 4 Esdras, Prayer of Manasses) are deliberately excluded from the default canonical list.

## Important distinction
This is the **Original Douay-Rheims**, not the later Challoner revision. It is also not the copyrighted Ignatius Catholic Study Bible / RSV-2CE. Arkforge does not bundle or reconstruct Ignatius Press study notes or RSV-2CE text.

## Layering policy
Source Scripture bytes are cached unchanged. Arkforge notes, questions, red-letter markings, Arthur synthesis, maps and commentary are separate records/layers with provenance labels.

## Release review
This file records the current engineering basis, not a legal opinion. Before commercial release, preserve the exact repository license snapshot/commit in the release evidence pack and complete final content-rights review.

**Red-letter boundary:** the source text is never rewritten; red-letter markings are reversible Arkforge display annotations stored separately from Scripture.


## V16 Jesus Study metadata
The life/ministry, parable, miracle, teaching, and title summaries in `faith/jesus-study-data.*` are original Arkforge study metadata consisting of references and short original summaries. They do not reproduce third-party study-Bible commentary.

Red-letter annotations are kept separate from Scripture text. V16 does not claim a complete phrase-level red-letter edition.
