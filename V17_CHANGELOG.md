# Faithbook V17 Change Log

## Identity
- Public app name changed to **Faithbook**.
- Android package namespace/applicationId remains `com.arkforge.faith` for continuity; that internal identifier is not the user-facing product name.
- Version: `17.0.0-humanity-ark`.

## Design
- Replaced the high-stimulation fantasy dashboard direction with Quiet Paper Covenant styling.
- Plain parchment / aged-leather visual language.
- Reduced shadows, gradients, animation, and reward-like visual cues.
- Primary navigation simplified to Home / Scripture / Understand / Garden / Live / Notes / Credits.

## Scripture
- Removed red-letter recoloring from the V17 public UI.
- Scripture is rendered without Faithbook recoloring or paraphrase.
- Jesus-speech study remains a separate reference/context concept.
- Seven Commentary Leaves renamed **Seven Context Leaves** in the public UI.

## Guide
- Removed Arthur from the public navigation/study UI.
- Added **Gabriel · Quiet Study Guide**, explicitly described as an interface helper named after the biblical messenger, not a claim that an angel is speaking through the app.

## Humanity Ark
Expanded starter data now includes:
- 74 named people/work records, with direct credit language and Scripture references,
- 30 peoples/groups records,
- 25 plant/context records,
- 20 food records,
- 8 oil/aromatic records,
- 10 textile/fiber records,
- 25 animal records,
- 20 materials/tools records,
- 18 tradition/practice records,
- 22 Civilization Garden domains.

People/work profiles now explicitly distinguish text attribution, translation questions, and inference. Skills are not treated as owned by the first named practitioner.

## Naming and credit
- Added KJV Naming Policy.
- Added Attribution & Credit Covenant.
- Built Credits & Sources screen.
- KJV naming metadata does not alter Scripture.

## Store truth
V17 remains source-level work. Signed AAB, physical-device acceptance and Play approval remain outstanding.
