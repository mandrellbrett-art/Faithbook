# Faithbook V18 Change Log — Ancestors

## Added
- New primary route: **Ancestors**.
- New home card: **Where Am I From?**
- Private local `ancestor_person` records.
- Private local `ancestor_relationship` records.
- Source/citation fields and five explicit confidence states.
- Fields for places, migration, occupations, languages, traditions, stories, and external genealogy references.
- Family-link types that preserve adoption, caregiving, step-family, and other relationships instead of forcing every family into one biological model.
- Biblical genealogy study links, kept explicitly separate from personal lineage claims.
- `faith/ancestors-spec.json` governance/specification file.

## Privacy and dignity
- No raw DNA import in this version.
- No racial-worth or ancestry-ranking model.
- Living relatives' information remains the user's responsibility to keep private/consensual.
- Ancestor records are intended for app-private local storage.

## Product identity
- Visible title/brand in the public shell is **Faithbook**.
- Existing Android namespace/applicationId continuity is not changed by this source edit.

## Verification status
This is a source candidate. A signed AAB, physical-phone acceptance, and Google Play approval remain unverified until actually built and tested.
