# ARK V25.4 — Whole-Phone Mirror Sweep

V25.4 combines Import All with a non-destructive longitudinal Mirror.

## Whole-phone scope
ARK indexes every shared-storage volume Android exposes through All Files Access and revisits previously authorized document-provider folders. It does not duplicate 500 GB of storage: the primary operation is metadata/link indexing.

Android still prevents ARK from silently reading another app's private `/data/data/<package>` sandbox.

## Mirror filtering
After Import All, ARK automatically runs a local-only Mirror Sweep.

Automatic archive signals are intentionally narrow:
- dad/father language combined with repeated conflict language,
- family language combined with repeated conflict language,
- repeated escape/relocation planning language.

High-emotion language without those combinations goes to **Review**, not automatic archive.

The filter does not diagnose the user, decide what actually happened, or delete source material.

## Principle
**Past me is evidence, not authority. Present me is an editor, not an executioner. Preserve the record, correct the boundary, keep the lesson.**

Default Global Search hides `MIRROR_ARCHIVE` phone items. The dedicated Mirror page can always recover them, override the classification, or attach a later reflection.
