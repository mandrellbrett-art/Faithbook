# HeritageFaith — Scripture Study Workspace

HeritageFaith is a public, local-first Christian Scripture study application. Its configured redistribution-safe Scripture foundation is tracked in `public-scripture-source.json`.

## Rule
Scripture is opened before commentary. HeritageFaith is a study tool, not clergy, an oracle, or a new source of revelation. Context and commentary remain separate records with provenance.

## Local study records
Notes, questions, Seven Commentary Leaves, maps, timelines, red-letter display annotations, and personal reflections are stored separately from Scripture source bytes.
