# Arkforge Faith — Scripture Study Workspace

Arkforge Faith is a public, local-first Catholic Scripture study application. Its built-in Scripture foundation is the rights-cleared Original Douay-Rheims dataset configured in `public-scripture-source.json`.

## Study records
Customers can create local notes, questions, resolution notes, church-group sessions, sermon notes, red-letter annotations, logic paths, and Seven Commentary Leaves. These records are created on the customer's device; no developer-owner personal records are seeded into the retail build.

## Source boundary
Third-party copyrighted study-Bible text, proprietary study notes, private scans, and DRM-protected content are not bundled. User-selected documents may be imported only through explicit Android document selection.

## Jesus-centered study
Scripture is opened before commentary. Arthur is a navigation/research helper; he is not presented as clergy, an oracle, or a source of doctrine.
