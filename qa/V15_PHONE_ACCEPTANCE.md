# Arkforge Faith V15 Physical-Phone Acceptance

Do not call V15 release-ready until these are tested on an Android phone.

- [ ] Fresh install launches as **Arkforge Faith** with no Termux dependency.
- [ ] Jesus-centered Home screen renders correctly in portrait, landscape, folded and unfolded layouts where applicable.
- [ ] Arthur floating guide opens, closes, routes to Scripture, Study, Worksheets, Living Church and Living Tradition.
- [ ] Tap **Install 73-book Scripture** on Wi-Fi; progress reaches 73/73 without duplicate or corrupt files.
- [ ] Force-close during Scripture download; reopen and confirm resume skips completed books and does not delete notes.
- [ ] After 73/73, disable network and open several OT, deuterocanonical and NT books offline.
- [ ] Verify modern book-name mapping: 1 Samuel → ODR 1 Kings; 1 Kings → ODR 3 Kings; Ezra → ODR 1 Esdras; Nehemiah → ODR 2 Esdras.
- [ ] Verify Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch and 1–2 Maccabees are present.
- [ ] Confirm 3 Esdras, 4 Esdras and Prayer of Manasses are not in the default 73-book selector.
- [ ] Search installed Scripture and inspect results from multiple books.
- [ ] Open source footnotes/cross-references and check layout on small screens.
- [ ] Add a verse note and question; restart app and confirm persistence.
- [ ] Add a red-letter annotation; toggle red-letter display and confirm Scripture source text itself remains unchanged.
- [ ] Verify Arthur Context does not overstate name matches as historical certainty.
- [ ] Open Seven Commentary Leaves, Church Group Book, 300 Worksheets, Living Church, Living Tradition, Sermons and Study Lab.
- [ ] Verify no owner-private data appears anywhere in public UI, exports, logs or backups.
- [ ] Test backup/restore on disposable customer data.
- [ ] Review Play Data Safety, privacy policy, rights evidence, store graphics, signing and $4.99 paid-app configuration.

Release gate: source checks alone do not satisfy these phone/store checks.
